# Ruth's Decision Patterns
*Started 13 August 2026. This document exists to capture recurring patterns in how Ruth actually makes decisions — not rules imposed on her, but observations of judgment already demonstrated across this project. Unlike WORKFLOW.md (process) or UNFLUMP_SPEC.md's Core Values (product principles), this is about the person making the decisions, and is meant to eventually outgrow this one project — likely extracted into its own standalone reference once there's enough real material to draw from, same treatment as the Development Workflow Principles.*

*Built incrementally, as real decisions happen — not written all at once. Each entry should trace back to an actual moment, not a general impression.*

## Observed patterns so far

**Precision over reassurance.** When a vague or comforting phrase is offered, Ruth consistently pushes for something clearer, even when the vague version is easier to accept in the moment. ("That's saved for you too, by the way" → rejected as unclear, even though it was gentler-sounding; "doesn't land on your legs" → corrected to the accurate physiological explanation rather than kept as a comforting myth.)

**Evidence over assumption, including correcting her own instinct.** Repeatedly asked for real research before accepting a claim, and was willing to have her own working assumption corrected by what the evidence actually showed (the recomposition-in-women claim, the exact whoosh mechanism, the joy-and-longevity framing narrowed to what was actually supportable).

**Resistance to anything paternalistic, even when well-intentioned.** Consistently caught and rejected persuasive or directive language, even in service of a good outcome (the MI-grounded language rules, "please reach out" flagged as an instruction, the guided-discovery onboarding design explicitly avoiding stated conclusions in favor of the user reaching them independently).

**Notices when something is technically correct but still feels wrong.** The clearest, most repeated pattern across the whole project — catching subtle experiential problems that pass functional testing (the vague-ack confusion, the mismatched grief/eating-disorder card, the silent-logging trust gap, the fourth-wall break). Usually surfaces as "that's not quite right" before being able to fully articulate why — the instinct arrives before the explanation.

**Willing to slow down and re-scope rather than ship something not fully stood-behind, even under real time pressure.** Held firm on proper safety investigation over quick patches multiple times, despite a genuinely tight timeline.

**Prefers building real, working things over polished narrative before it's true.** Explicitly deferred marketing and content creation until the product itself is something she can honestly stand behind — "no point starting until I have a product I can stand behind."

## How this gets built

At natural session-close points, add any new decision moment that reveals a genuine pattern — not every decision, only ones that add real signal about how Ruth thinks, distinct from the specific product or process choice itself.
